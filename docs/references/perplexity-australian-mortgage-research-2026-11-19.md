# Australian Residential Mortgage Document Collection: Public Primary-Source Evidence & Broker Intake Research

**Research Date:** 19 November 2026  
**Scope:** Publicly accessible primary lender documentation across **Commonwealth Bank (CBA)**, **NAB**, **ANZ**, **Westpac**, and **ING**, official **Australian Taxation Office (ATO)** guidance, and public **Australian mortgage broker checklists** (Mortgage Choice, Aussie, Home Loan Experts, Lendi, Loan Market, GNT Finance, Investors Choice Mortgages, Felix Finance).  
**Purpose:** Evidence base for designing a conditional **initial document collection checklist** (intake UX/workflow), strictly separated from credit assessment, serviceability calculation, or lender credit decisions.

---

## 1. Executive Summary (Key Findings First)

- **No universal lender document rule exists in Australia:** Public evidence proves that document requirements vary sharply by lender, channel (retail branch/direct vs accredited broker), existing-customer status, loan-to-value ratio (LVR <=80% vs >80% Lenders Mortgage Insurance [LMI]), and assessment pathway. Over-collecting upfront creates borrower friction—and in specific cases actively harms processing (for example, submitting full financial statements to Westpac disqualifies an eligible self-employed borrower from its 2-NOA **Fast Track** queue and forces full assessment, per [Westpac Broker Self-Employed Hub](https://www.westpac.com.au/brokers/ideas-hub/self-employed/)).
- **Lenders leave outdated PDFs live alongside 2025–2026 policy:** Several major lenders still host pre-CCR or pre-STP PDFs on public URLs—most notably [ING's Mortgage Application Checklist (`ING IM267 | 08/17`, PDF p. 1)](https://www.ing.com.au/pdf/application_checklist.pdf) from August 2017 and [Westpac's Retail Application Checklist (`WBCHFM001 0920`, PDF pp. 1–4)](https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/wbc/documents/pdf/pb/Home_Loan_Application_Checklist.pdf) from May 2020. Comparing Westpac's May 2020 retail PDF against [Westpac's February 2026 Broker Minimum Required Documents Checklist (`WBC22133 0226`, PDF pp. 1–8)](https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/brokers/documents/applications/wbc-minimum-required-documents-checklist.pdf) shows major shifts: upfront Comprehensive Credit Reporting (CCR) has replaced routine 6-month refinance statement collection for most Westpac broker applications, payslip freshness widened from 1 month to 2 months from formal approval, and 1-year / 2-NOA self-employed pathways were introduced.
- **History coverage, file count, and freshness are three separate dimensions:** Intake checklists routinely conflate them. For example, **6 months of home loan history** does not mean 6 monthly PDFs—at CBA, home loan statements are issued semi-annually in January and July ([CBA Guide to Home Loan Statements](https://www.commbank.com.au/home-loans/guide-to-statements.html)), meaning a borrower must supply **1 official statement** (proving account ownership, name, and address) **plus 1 internet banking transaction printout** bridging to the current date ([Home Loan Experts Statements Guide](https://www.homeloanexperts.com.au/home-loan-documents/statements-transaction-histories/)).
- **The "3 vs 6 months of credit-card statements" contradiction resolves into four distinct evidentiary tiers:**
  1. **0 statements (Upfront CCR / Internal accounts):** Used by [Westpac Broker (`WBC22133 0226`, PDF pp. 5, 7)](https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/brokers/documents/applications/wbc-minimum-required-documents-checklist.pdf) via 24-month CCR on ApplyOnline, [ANZ Retail Checklist](https://www.anz.com.au/personal/home-loans/get-started/checklist/) via Experian Credit Report, and [CBA Retail Checklist](https://www.commbank.com.au/home-loans/applying-for-a-home-loan.html) for internal CommBank cards.
  2. **1 most recent statement (Retained / continuing external cards):** Standard lender requirement to verify the **credit limit** (assessed at ~3.8% per month regardless of balance) when the card is kept open—explicit in [NAB's Home Loan Application (`79936A-0725`, PDF p. 2)](https://www.nab.com.au/content/dam/nabrwd/documents/forms/loans/home-loan-application-form.pdf) and [Home Loan Experts' Statements Guide](https://www.homeloanexperts.com.au/home-loan-documents/statements-transaction-histories/).
  3. **3 consecutive months of statements (Standard credit-card debt consolidation OR blanket broker intake):** Required when consolidating/refinancing credit or store cards in [Mortgage Choice SmartSelect Checklist (PDF p. 4)](https://www.mortgagechoice.com.au/media/4488223/supporting-document-checklist.pdf), [ING Increasing Your Loan Hints (`INGD_IM301`, PDF p. 1)](https://www.ing.com.au/assets/pdf/INGD_IM301_Handy_Hints_Increasing_your_loan.pdf), [ING Application Checklist (`ING IM267 | 08/17`, PDF p. 1)](https://www.ing.com.au/pdf/application_checklist.pdf), and [Macquarie Cover Sheet](https://www.homeloanexperts.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/Macquarie-Bank-Application-cover-sheet.pdf), and collected upfront across all cards by blanket broker checklists ([Aussie Supporting Documents](https://www.aussie.com.au/about-us/supporting-documents/); [Home Loan Experts 2026 Checklist](https://www.homeloanexperts.com.au/home-loan-documents/)).
  4. **6 consecutive months of statements (Conservative broker refinance / high-LVR or non-CCR debt consolidation):** Requested by conservative broker refinance checklists ([Investors Choice Mortgages](https://investorschoice.com.au/home-loan-process/); [Felix Finance Refinancer Checklist](https://felixfinance.com.au/articles/mortgage-application-document-checklist); [Home Loan Partners 2026 Debt Consolidation Guide](https://homeloanpartners.com.au/2026/07/24/refinance-to-consolidate-debt-in-australia-the-2026-strategic-guide/)) to align unsecured-debt conduct checks with the 6-month mortgage repayment window where card issuers do not report full CCR history, where LVR exceeds 80% (LMI), or where specialist lenders are used.
- **Public sources leave specific gaps gated behind accredited broker portals:** Exact variable-income shading/tenure rules (commission, bonus, overtime tiers), Open Banking / CDR read-only bank-feed acceptance, post-May 15 tax-return rollover exceptions, HECS/HELP balance verification, Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) closure proofs, and private-key lender policy manuals sit inside gated portals (CBA CommBroker, NAB Broker, ANZ Broker Hub, Westpac BrokerHub, ING Introducer Portal, and aggregator software such as ApplyOnline and Mercury Nexus).

## 3. Australian Document Terminology, Official Names & Industry Aliases

Designing a clear Australian intake portal requires mapping colloquial terms, legacy employer labels, and official ATO/banking names so applicants upload the right file on the first attempt.

| Canonical Document Concept | Official Current Name | Common Australian Aliases / Legacy Terms | Critical Verification & Format Nuances |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Employee Pay Advice** | **Payslip** (YTD or Non-YTD) | Pay advice, wage slip, remittance advice | Must show employee name, employer name, **employer ABN** (if missing at ING, a salary-credit bank statement becomes mandatory per [ING `IM450 | 12/25`, PDF p. 10](https://www.ing.com.au/pdf/HomeLoans/Variation/VariationFormG.pdf)), pay period dates, gross/net pay, and Year-to-Date (YTD) figure where relied upon. |
| **Annual Payroll Tax Summary** | **ATO Income Statement** (marked **"Tax ready"** after FY end, or in-year YTD via myGov) | **PAYG Payment Summary**, **PAYG Summary**, **Group Certificate** (pre-2000 term still printed on [ING `IM450 | 12/25`, PDF p. 10](https://www.ing.com.au/pdf/HomeLoans/Variation/VariationFormG.pdf) and [Home Loan Experts Refinance Guide](https://www.homeloanexperts.com.au/refinance-home-loan/refinance-documents/)) | Under **Single Touch Payroll (STP)**, employers no longer issue paper/PDF PAYG Payment Summaries; borrowers download their **Income Statement** via **myGov -> ATO online services -> Employment -> Income statement** ([ATO `QC58694`](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/jobs-and-employment-types/working-as-an-employee/income-statements/access-your-income-statement)). Employers have until **14 July** to mark prior-FY statements **"Tax ready"**. |
| **Individual Tax Filing** | **Individual Income Tax Return (ITR)** | Personal tax return, full tax return | Accountant-prepared or myGov self-lodged tax return (all pages, not just the cover summary). Note: **TFNs must be redacted/removed** before submission (explicitly mandated on p. 1 of [Westpac `WBC22133 0226`](https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/brokers/documents/applications/wbc-minimum-required-documents-checklist.pdf)). |
| **ATO Tax Assessment** | **ATO Notice of Assessment (NOA)** | Tax assessment notice, ATO assessment | Official ATO-issued notice confirming taxable income and tax payable/refundable after an Individual ITR is processed. Retrieved from myGov inbox. Companies do **not** receive an individual-style NOA in the same workflow; [NAB `79936A-0725` (PDF p. 2)](https://www.nab.com.au/content/dam/nabrwd/documents/forms/loans/home-loan-application-form.pdf) specifies **Company Tax Portal** printout alongside NOA, while [CBA Retail Checklist](https://www.commbank.com.au/home-loans/applying-for-a-home-loan.html) accepts an **accountant's letter confirming final returns have been lodged with the ATO**. |
| **Business Entity Financials & Tax Filings** | **Company / Partnership / Trust Income Tax Return** + **Financial Statements (Profit & Loss Statement and Balance Sheet)** | Business financials, P&L and balance sheet, trading accounts, Trustee tax return | Includes Depreciation Schedule, Notes to Financials, and director/beneficiary distributions. Trusts additionally require a **certified copy of the executed/stamped Trust Deed** (plus any variation deeds) ([Westpac `WBC22133 0226`, PDF p. 4](https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/brokers/documents/applications/wbc-minimum-required-documents-checklist.pdf); [ING `IM267`, PDF p. 1](https://www.ing.com.au/pdf/application_checklist.pdf)). |
| **Indirect / Interim Tax Filing** | **Business Activity Statement (BAS)** | Quarterly BAS, monthly BAS, IAS (Instalment Activity Statement), ATO Running Balance Account (RBA) portal printout | Used to evidence interim/current-year trading turnover, deteriorating performance checks ([NAB `79936A-0725`, PDF p. 2](https://www.nab.com.au/content/dam/nabrwd/documents/forms/loans/home-loan-application-form.pdf)), or >60% YoY profit growth sustainability ([Westpac Self-Employed Hub](https://www.westpac.com.au/brokers/ideas-hub/self-employed/)). Must show ATO lodgement receipt or portal history, not unlodged draft worksheets. |
| **Bank Account Evidence** | **Official Bank Statement** vs **Internet Banking Transaction History / Transaction Listing** | Salary-credit statement, eStatement, transaction summary, proof of balance report | **Crucial operational distinction** ([Home Loan Experts Statements Guide](https://www.homeloanexperts.com.au/home-loan-documents/statements-transaction-histories/)): An **official statement** displays bank logo, **account holder full name, residential address**, account/BSB number, opening/closing balances, and period transactions. An **internet transaction history/listing** shows recent transactions and running balance with bank branding/account number, **often without name/address**. **CSV/Excel exports and cropped mobile screenshots are rejected.** When statements issue quarterly or semi-annually, borrowers must pair **1 official statement + 1 continuous PDF transaction listing**. |
| **Property Ownership & Liability Proof** | **Council Rates Notice** & **Comprehensive Credit Report (CCR)** | Rates notice, water rates, Valuer-General Notice of Valuation; Experian / Equifax / Illion credit file | Council rates notice (showing street address, owner names, and Lot/Plan title reference) is universally used across refinances and existing properties ([CBA Checklist](https://www.commbank.com.au/home-loans/applying-for-a-home-loan.html); [Westpac `WBC22133 0226`, PDF p. 1](https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/brokers/documents/applications/wbc-minimum-required-documents-checklist.pdf)). Upfront **CCR** captures 24 months of Repayment History Information (RHI) and credit limits ([Westpac `WBC22133 0226`, PDF p. 5](https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/brokers/documents/applications/wbc-minimum-required-documents-checklist.pdf)). |

## 6. Alternatives & Contradictions in Public Evidence

### 6.1 Deep Dive: Resolving the "3 vs 6 Months of Credit-Card Statements" Contradiction

Across Australian public sources, credit-card statement requirements appear contradictory—ranging from **zero** to **six months**. First-principles decomposition shows that lenders and brokers are testing **two different risks**:
1. **Serviceability commitment (Retained Cards):** Under APRA/NCCP serviceability rules, an open credit card is assessed on its **total credit limit** (commonly ~3.8% of the limit per month), even if the balance is $0 ([Mortgage Choice Debt Consolidation Guide](https://www.mortgagechoice.com.au/paul.wrensted/our-services/refinancing-debt-consolidation/); [ZedPlus Debt Consolidation](https://zedplus.net.au/insights/home-loan-debt-consolidation/)). Verifying the limit requires only **CCR data (0 statements)** or **1 latest statement**.
2. **Repayment conduct & payout integrity (Consolidated Cards):** When rolling unsecured credit-card debt into a 25–30 year residential mortgage, the lender removes the card's monthly limit commitment from post-settlement serviceability **only if** the card has clean repayment conduct (no arrears, over-limit fees, or cash-advance distress), is paid out at settlement, and is **closed (or limit-reduced)**.

Why public checklists split between **0, 1, 3, and 6 months**:

**Settlement / Post-Approval Condition Difference (Consolidated vs Retained Cards):**
- **Retained card:** No closure document required; full limit stays in serviceability. (If the borrower voluntarily lowers the limit to pass serviceability, a **credit-limit reduction confirmation** from the card issuer is required prior to unconditional/settlement).
- **Consolidated card:** Requires BPAY/account payout details on the statement so the incoming lender can disburse funds directly at settlement, **plus** borrower election to close the facility ([ING `IM450 | 12/25`, p. 19](https://www.ing.com.au/pdf/HomeLoans/Variation/VariationFormG.pdf)) and a **written account closure / $0 limit confirmation letter** as a settlement or post-settlement condition ([Mortgage Choice Debt Consolidation](https://www.mortgagechoice.com.au/paul.wrensted/our-services/refinancing-debt-consolidation/)). Note also that **standalone unsecured debt consolidation** (where no external mortgage is being refinanced) is **ineligible for CBA FASTRefi®** ([CBA FASTRefi Fact Sheet, p. 2](https://www.commbank.com.au/content/dam/commbank-assets/home-buying/docs/006-092-refinance-fact-sheet.pdf)).

## 7. Accredited Broker Policy Gaps (Not Verifiable from Public Sources Alone)

Public websites and retail/broker flyers omit detailed underwriting rules that sit inside **accredited broker portals** (CommBroker, NAB Broker, ANZ Broker Hub, Westpac BrokerHub, ING Introducer Portal) and aggregator engines. A document-collection system **must not guess** these rules:

1. **Open Banking (CDR) & Digital Bank-Statement Feeds (Illion BankStatements / Cashdeck / NextGen):** Public pages rarely disclose which lenders waive PDF transaction/savings/refinance statements when a borrower completes an encrypted CDR/Open Banking read-only connection, versus which LMI providers still mandate native PDF statements.
2. **Exact Variable PAYG Income Tiers (Bonus, Commission, Overtime, Allowances, RSU/Bonus Shares):** Outside Westpac's non-base payslip note (`WBC22133 0226`, p. 3) and Emergency Services policy, public sources do not publish whether CBA, NAB, ANZ, or ING require 6 months, 12 months, or 24 months of commission/bonus evidence, whether employer letters are mandatory, or how YTD vs prior-FY figures are shaded.
3. **Probation, Recent Job Change, Contractor (PAYG vs ABN) & Parental Leave Return-to-Work Docs:** Public checklists mention "employment contract or employer letter" generally, but omit exact return-to-work employer letter wording (start date, guaranteed hours, base salary, savings buffer during unpaid leave) and daily-rate IT/medical contractor rules.
4. **ATO Tax-Agent Lodgement Extensions & Post-May 15 Cutoff Nuances:** Westpac (`WBC22133 0226`, p. 4) notes that after 15 May, prior June 30 financials are mandatory, while NAB (`79936A-0725`, p. 2) cites 21/24 months and ANZ cites 22.5/24 months. Exact calendar rollover dates for tax-agent-extended clients (and whether ATO Tax Portal debt/integrated client account printouts showing $0 ATO debt are mandatory for all self-employed borrowers) sit in gated credit policy.
5. ** Debt Consolidation LVR Caps, Debt Count Limits & Direct-Payout vs Cash-Out Rules:** Public guides do not specify maximum LVR ceilings for consolidating unsecured debt (often capped at 80%–85% LVR or limited to <=4–5 facilities), nor when a lender mandates solicitor/settlement cheque payout + prior-to-settlement card closure versus post-settlement borrower closure.
6. **ING Current Initial Broker Checklist (`IM266` / Introducer Policy):** Because ING gates its current broker introducer guides on `broker.ing.com.au` while leaving the August 2017 `IM267` PDF on `ing.com.au`, accredited broker access is required to verify whether ING has updated its initial-application CCR/refinance statement rules since 2017.
7. **HECS/HELP, ATO Payment Plans, Novated Leases & Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL):** Public checklists mention disclosing HECS/HELP and BNPL (Afterpay/Zip), but do not specify when a myGov ATO HELP loan balance screenshot, novated lease pre/post-tax salary-sacrifice schedule, or BNPL closure screenshot is mandatory.

### 8.2 Unresolved Policy & Product Questions Before Building the Checklist

Before locking conditional logic into a broker intake portal or CRM workflow, resolve these ten operational questions with the brokerage's compliance/credit team:

1. **Upfront CCR & Open Banking Integration:** Will the brokerage pull an upfront Comprehensive Credit Report (Equifax/Experian/Illion) and/or use an Open Banking/bank-statement aggregator (e.g., Illion BankStatements / NextGen) during initial intake? (Determines whether transaction and retained-liability PDF uploads can default to conditional exceptions rather than mandatory uploads.)
2. **Panel Breadth (Prime Majors vs Non-Bank / Specialist Lenders):** Is the intake workflow serving prime residential borrowers only, or also specialist/non-conforming debt-consolidation borrowers (e.g., Pepper Money, Liberty, La Trobe)? (Determines whether consolidated credit cards should request **3 months** by default or **6 months** when arrears/LMI/specialist flags are ticked.)
