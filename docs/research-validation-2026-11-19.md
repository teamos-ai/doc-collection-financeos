# Australian document research · validation pass

19 November 2026. This supplements, rather than rewrites, the 18 November research and retained catalogue. Public pages and supplied local PDFs were read. No accredited broker portal policy was claimed inspected.

## Outcome

Enough evidence exists to build the visual specification and distinguish scenario routes. It does not support a universal lender checklist or automatic issue of every proposed rule. Preserve 6 months for home-loan refinance, 6 for consolidated cards and 3 for retained cards as fixed founder collection standards. No broker override.

## Sources and limitations

- **CBA — [CommBank · application checklist](https://www.commbank.com.au/home-loans/applying-for-a-home-loan.html)**. Web page checked 19 Nov 2026. Primary source. Salary-credit evidence distinguishes three months from six for casual employment. Self-employed evidence and rental alternatives vary. This supports conditional requests, not a blanket bank-statement pack.

- **ANZ — [ANZ · home loan checklist](https://www.anz.com.au/personal/home-loans/get-started/checklist/)**. Web page checked 19 Nov 2026 · excludes ANZ Plus. Primary source. PAYG evidence depends on YTD history. Recent payslips may be up to 60 days old. Self-employed routes include one-year evidence or eligible company wages. Home-loan statements are generally unnecessary, with exceptions. Signed contracts come after approval-in-principle.

- **NAB — [NAB · application, page 2](https://www.nab.com.au/content/dam/nabrwd/documents/forms/loans/home-loan-application-form.pdf)**. Document 79936A-0725 · July 2025 · checked 19 Nov 2026. Dated primary PDF. Refinanced loans: six months of transactions, evidence no older than 90 days. PAYG alternatives include one YTD payslip or two of the last three, usually within 45 days; longer pay cycles differ. This separates history, quantity and freshness.

- **WBC — [Westpac · self-employed broker guide](https://www.westpac.com.au/brokers/ideas-hub/self-employed/)**. Web page checked 19 Nov 2026. Primary source. Separate eligible Fast Track, one-year and standard two-year routes. Fast Track uses two personal ATO notices of assessment. Other routes can require entity financials. Do not silently put excluded standalone financials back into the collection catalogue.

- **ING — [ING · mortgage application checklist](https://www.ing.com.au/pdf/application_checklist.pdf)**. Historical 2017 checklist · re-located 19 Nov 2026. Historical only. Useful Australian naming and older six-month loan / three-month card refinance references. Not accepted as current policy. Current detailed ING broker requirements remain an explicit gap; a newer broker-flyer URL could not be retrieved in this pass.

- **ATO-I — [ATO · income statements](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/jobs-and-employment-types/working-as-an-employee/income-statements/access-your-income-statement)**. Checked 19 Nov 2026. Terminology authority. Use ATO income statement. A PAYG payment summary is an alternative employer reporting document, not a tax return or notice of assessment. Keep the actual evidence route on the request.

- **ATO-N — [ATO · notice of assessment](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/your-tax-return/your-notice-of-assessment)**. Checked 19 Nov 2026. Terminology authority. The notice of assessment is the ATO assessment following a tax return. Request the correct financial year and keep it distinct from both the return and the ATO account statement.

- **HG — [Hunter Galloway · supplied checklist](https://www.huntergalloway.com.au/)**. Local one-page PDF read 19 Nov 2026 · publication date unknown. Broker comparison. Compares common income, savings, liabilities and property evidence. Its three-year self-employed / six-month savings suggestions differ from the proposed baseline. Use as gap discovery, not lender policy. Original file is recorded in the research handoff.

- **AUSSIE — [Aussie · supplied refinance checklist](https://www.aussie.com.au/)**. Local one-page PDF ©2021 read 19 Nov 2026. Historical comparison. Separates refinance purpose, existing debts, income and rates notices. Its older “Group Certificates” wording maps to the appropriate ATO income statement / PAYG payment summary route. Expense evidence stays outside this module.

- **ANZ-C — [ANZ · supplied construction checklist](https://www.anz.com.au/personal/home-loans/)**. Local PDF 08.2022; internally current at 29 Apr 2020. Historical stage comparison. Deposit, progress and final draw evidence differ. Use it to justify a construction-stage input; do not import historical payment lead times or request final-stage evidence at initial application.

## Conflicts resolved explicitly

- Founder exclusions remain. Lender requests for expenses or standalone business financials are handled outside this module; exclusion is not a claim lenders never assess them.
- ANZ and NAB publish different refinance evidence routes. Keep the founder standard separate from bank policy.
- Hunter Galloway’s undated comparison suggests longer self-employed and savings histories. It does not automatically override the proposed common pack.
- One-year / streamlined company, partnership and trust income routes must be selected and checked, not stacked on top of the standard pack.
- Statement history, document count and age are different fields. File count never validates coverage.
- The supplied construction PDF is stage-based and historical. Its payment timeframes are not adopted.
- Native HighLevel actions exist, but request-scoped access, authoritative queries and safe concurrent-loan behaviour remain implementation tests.
