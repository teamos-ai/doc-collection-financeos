# Australian document research · validation pass

Started 19 November; updated 20 November 2026 after the [completed Perplexity cross-check](perplexity-cross-check-2026-11-20.md). This supplements, rather than rewrites, the 18 November research and retained catalogue. Public pages and supplied local PDFs were read. No accredited broker portal policy was claimed inspected.

## Outcome

Enough evidence exists to build the visual specification and distinguish scenario routes. It does not support a universal lender checklist or automatic issue of every proposed rule. Preserve 6 months for home-loan refinance, 6 for consolidated cards and 3 for retained cards as fixed founder collection standards. No broker override.

## Sources and limitations

- **CBA — [CommBank · application checklist](https://www.commbank.com.au/home-loans/applying-for-a-home-loan.html)**. Web page checked 19 Nov 2026. Primary source. Salary-credit evidence distinguishes three months from six for casual employment. Self-employed evidence and rental alternatives vary. This supports conditional requests, not a blanket bank-statement pack.

- **ANZ — [ANZ · home loan checklist](https://www.anz.com.au/personal/home-loans/get-started/checklist/)**. Web page checked 19 Nov 2026 · excludes ANZ Plus. Primary source. PAYG evidence depends on YTD history. Recent payslips may be up to 60 days old. Self-employed routes include one-year evidence or eligible company wages. Home-loan statements are generally unnecessary, with exceptions. Signed contracts come after approval-in-principle.

- **NAB — [NAB · application, page 2](https://www.nab.com.au/content/dam/nabrwd/documents/forms/loans/home-loan-application-form.pdf)**. Document 79936A-0725 · July 2025 · checked 19 Nov 2026. Dated primary PDF. Refinanced loans: six months of transactions, evidence no older than 90 days. PAYG alternatives include one YTD payslip or two of the last three, usually within 45 days; longer pay cycles differ. This separates history, quantity and freshness.

- **WBC — [Westpac · self-employed broker guide](https://www.westpac.com.au/brokers/ideas-hub/self-employed/)**. Web page checked 19 Nov 2026. Primary source. Separate eligible Fast Track, one-year and standard two-year routes. Fast Track uses two personal ATO notices of assessment. Other routes can require entity financials. Do not silently put excluded standalone financials back into the collection catalogue.

- **ING — [ING · mortgage application checklist](https://www.ing.com.au/pdf/application_checklist.pdf)**. Historical 2017 checklist · re-located 19 Nov 2026. Historical only. Useful Australian naming and older six-month loan / three-month card refinance references. Not accepted as current policy. Current initial broker requirements remain a gap. ING-V is a newer variation form, not a replacement initial-application checklist.

- **ATO-I — [ATO · income statements](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/jobs-and-employment-types/working-as-an-employee/income-statements/access-your-income-statement)**. Checked 19 Nov 2026. Terminology authority. Use ATO income statement. A PAYG payment summary is an alternative employer reporting document, not a tax return or notice of assessment. Keep the actual evidence route on the request.

- **ATO-N — [ATO · notice of assessment](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/your-tax-return/your-notice-of-assessment)**. Checked 19 Nov 2026. Terminology authority. The notice of assessment is the ATO assessment following a tax return. Request the correct financial year and keep it distinct from both the return and the ATO account statement.

- **HG — [Hunter Galloway · supplied checklist](https://www.huntergalloway.com.au/)**. Local one-page PDF read 19 Nov 2026 · publication date unknown. Broker comparison. Compares common income, savings, liabilities and property evidence. Its three-year self-employed / six-month savings suggestions differ from the proposed baseline. Use as gap discovery, not lender policy. Original file is recorded in the research handoff.

- **AUSSIE — [Aussie · supplied refinance checklist](https://www.aussie.com.au/)**. Local one-page PDF ©2021 read 19 Nov 2026. Historical comparison. Separates refinance purpose, existing debts, income and rates notices. Its older “Group Certificates” wording maps to the appropriate ATO income statement / PAYG payment summary route. Expense evidence stays outside this module.

- **ANZ-C — [ANZ · supplied construction checklist](https://www.anz.com.au/content/dam/anzcomau/documents/pdf/construction-loan-checklist.pdf)**. Local PDF 08.2022; internally current at 29 Apr 2020. Historical stage comparison. Deposit, progress and final draw evidence differ. Use it to justify a construction-stage input; do not import historical payment lead times or request final-stage evidence at initial application.

- **WBC-26 — [Westpac · broker minimum documents](https://www.westpac.com.au/content/dam/public/brokers/documents/applications/wbc-minimum-required-documents-checklist.pdf)**. WBC22133 0226 · February 2026 · checked 20 Nov 2026. Broker PDF · channel-specific. Pages 1, 3–8: TFN removal, PAYG alternatives, CCR-based liability/refinance evidence, staged building documents. Exceptions still refer to credit policy; a waiver is not automatic.

- **ING-V — [ING · existing home-loan variation](https://www.ing.com.au/pdf/HomeLoans/Variation/VariationFormG.pdf)**. IM450 12/25 · December 2025 · checked 20 Nov 2026. Existing-loan variations only. Pages 1/10/19: applies to existing ING loan changes; applicable Section I uses a payslip under one month and salary-credit evidence if ABN is absent. Closure Yes/No is an election, not proof of a universal closure-letter requirement.

- **CBA-S — [CommBank · statement issue frequency](https://www.commbank.com.au/home-loans/guide-to-statements.html)**. Checked 20 Nov 2026. Primary source. Home-loan statements issue in January and July. Request date coverage, not six monthly files. A statement closing balance is not a payout figure.

- **HLE-S — [Home Loan Experts · statements guide](https://www.homeloanexperts.com.au/home-loan-documents/statements-transaction-histories/)**. Checked 20 Nov 2026 · publication date not independently established. Broker practice, not universal policy. Distinguishes official statements from transaction histories and recommends pairing when ownership details/history are missing. Its CSV restriction is broker guidance, not a verified all-lender upload-format ban.

- **ATO-D — [ATO · definitions](https://www.ato.gov.au/Definitions/?anchor=P789-67151)**. Official indexed definition checked 20 Nov 2026; direct page returned 403. Official indexed terminology. IAS is similar to BAS but excludes GST and some other taxes. BAS, IAS and ATO account ledgers are not aliases; only BAS remains in this catalogue.

- **ATO-STP — [ATO · finalising STP data](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/hiring-and-paying-your-workers/single-touch-payroll/finalising-your-stp-data)**. Official indexed guidance checked 20 Nov 2026. Terminology authority. Payment summaries remain relevant for payments not reported/finalised through STP. Keep completed-year finalisation distinct from current YTD evidence; do not require an unfinished financial year to be Tax ready.

- **WBC-P — [Westpac · public broker policy](https://www.westpac.com.au/brokers/policy/)**. Checked 20 Nov 2026. Conflicting public guidance. The page mentions both three and six months of rental history for genuine savings. Keep this purpose separate from landlord rental income; resolve the applicable route before issue.

- **ANZ-P — [ANZ · paper application](https://www.anz.com.au/content/dam/anzcomau/documents/pdf/home-loan-application-form.pdf)**. Page 2 · checked 20 Nov 2026. Channel-specific form. Lists three months of home-loan statements if applicable. The web checklist generally does not require them. Do not erase this conditional channel difference.

- **ANZ-D — [ANZ · general documents guide](https://www.anz.com.au/personal/home-loans/get-started/documents-home-loan-application/)**. Checked 20 Nov 2026. General guide / differing freshness. Mentions self-employed tax evidence under 18 months; the detailed checklist uses different limits. No universal tax-age cutoff is adopted.

- **CBA-B — [CommBank · business-owner verification](https://www.commbank.com.au/business/home-loans.html)**. Checked 20 Nov 2026. Primary source · eligible routes. Simplified verification pairs salary evidence with business-profit evidence or an accepted accountant letter. It is not the same as ANZ’s company-wage route or Westpac’s NOA-only route.

HG and AUSSIE are supplied local comparisons, not current lender policy. ATO pages that rejected direct access are identified as indexed-text checks; no full-page audit is claimed. Publication date and check date are separate.

## Conflicts resolved explicitly

- Founder exclusions remain. Lender requests for expenses or standalone business financials are handled outside this module; exclusion is not a claim lenders never assess them.
- ANZ and NAB publish different refinance evidence routes. Keep the founder standard separate from bank policy.
- Hunter Galloway’s undated comparison suggests longer self-employed and savings histories. It does not automatically override the proposed common pack.
- One-year / streamlined company, partnership and trust income routes must be selected and checked, not stacked on top of the standard pack.
- Statement history, document count and age are different fields. File count never validates coverage.
- The supplied construction PDF is stage-based and historical. Its payment timeframes are not adopted.
- Native HighLevel actions exist, but request-scoped access, authoritative queries and safe concurrent-loan behaviour remain implementation tests.
